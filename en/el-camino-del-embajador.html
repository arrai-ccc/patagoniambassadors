The experience

You stop being a visitor.
You become an ambassador.

It’s a real immersion in the territory — and the best way to know whether this place is yours. You walk the trails, sleep under the Aysén sky, read the land with those who know it, and return changed by it.

Day 1

Threshold

The slow road inward. The first silence. The signal fades — and that is the gift.

Day 2

Reading the land

Trails to the lagoons with the Avellano Guides. The land stops being scenery and becomes relationship.

Day 3

Your place

Time alone at a point in the park. One question: what would it mean to protect this, forever?

Day 4

Integration

A closing circle at dawn. You name what changed. The departure is slow, on purpose.

The moment you don’t forget

"The land chooses you, not the other way around."

A private relationship with a piece of real land, protected in perpetuity. It’s not a view — it’s a place that becomes yours to care for.

From the journey to home

If after your journey you decide to stay, we talk about it: the trip is the first step of your path as an ambassador.

The experience that brought you here becomes part of your home.
